How the dogs show the fungus

Field card — remaining five

This is not mysticism. Watch the dogs. They are the instrument.

What to watch

  • Sudden focus on one object, corner, vent or fabric — then destruction or removal.
  • Repeated attention to mouth, eyes, nose and other openings.
  • Coat change after a new building, car, heater or wet room.
  • Refusal to settle in a room that looks “clean”.
  • Obsession with a person’s face after that person has been in another building.

What the organism is doing

  • Gathering where air is still, warm, wet or recirculated.
  • Hiding in dust, fabric, heaters, copper and other dense surfaces.
  • Transferring through hard material — wiping one spot is not the end.
  • Adapting fast; one survivor is enough to restart.
  • Fogging human attention so the host keeps living inside the colony.

What to do in the next hour

  1. Leave the worst room with the dogs.
  2. Photograph what they targeted before you move it.
  3. Bag and isolate that item.
  4. Kill moisture first: dry, vent, stop the leak.
  5. Hard surfaces only: long-contact vinegar or 3% hydrogen peroxide, then wipe the film off.
  6. Porous stuff they flagged: bag it and get it out.
  7. Change clothes. Wipe dog paws and coats.
  8. Write three lines: date, what the dog did, what you removed.

What not to do

  • Do not start a total war with fogging, bleach and rage.
  • Do not drag flagged belongings into the next hotel, car or house.
  • Do not treat brain fog as laziness.
  • Do not wait for a department to believe you before you move the dogs.

Hostile strains get containment. The rest get respect. Follow the dogs.
