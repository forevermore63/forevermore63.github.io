Origin, Line, and the Fungus Compact

Recorded 31 August 2026 — Emily Blue Richards

Forevermore Farm / Noosa Dachshunds / Sausage Therapy

These dogs are sentient family and working cancer-detection animals. This page holds the origin so it does not have to be spoken from zero again.

1. How the line began

Sausage arrived in 2019 as COVID broke. That was not a casual pet purchase. It was the first visible result of a life plan: train dachshunds. Ten years of searching came before him. Another ten years went into travelling the country to select sires and bitches.

The pack was built as a cancer-detection line that also locates and disrupts environmental mould and microbes. At its height there were 23 of these dogs. Five remain. They have been stolen six times. Recovery attempts have included being shot at.

Tom is named after a late brother. Sausage died during a police raid that found nothing. Pepper’s son, Sausage’s grandson, is in the remaining five.

2. How the dogs were chosen

Temperament first; cream longhair silky coat; low-allergy coat; no IVDD and no stacked degenerative markers; no piebald, merle, dilution or dapple. Those mutations are recent. Historical colours were kept. Coat change tracked cancer-mould load.

3. What the dogs were actually doing

They selected objects and destroyed or removed them. They obsessed over mouth, eyes, nose and other openings. They were pointing at fungus so camouflaged that most people never see it until the damage is done.

4. How the organism was identified

Not a ghost, spirit, karma, unicorn, CIA plot or anarchy story. It is fungus. Method: microscope, macro camera, footage, capture, repeat measure, journalism training, bloodwork, mould specialist, Morgellons-spectrum label. Working description: amoeboid fungus, plant–animal connecting form, dual reproduction, one survivor can adapt. Transfer includes skin and openings into a copper ball and out as a needle of light. Video exists.

5. The compact

Total war failed. If it intended simple kill it had years. Two tracks: contain hostile strains in no-go zones; work with capable strains. The dogs already do this.

6. What Sausage Therapy does

  • Keep the remaining five as family and workers.
  • Teach what they have already shown.
  • Hold the evidence file.
  • Refuse to restart from zero.
