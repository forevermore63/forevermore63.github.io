Sausage Therapy

Why these dogs exist

Sausage came in 2019 as the world locked down. He was the first dog in a plan that had already taken ten years of searching and another ten years of travelling Australia to choose the right sires and bitches.

They were meant to be emotional-support dachshunds. They became something more.

They are cancer-detection dogs who also find mould and microbial mass in the rooms people actually live in. They point at it. They tear it down. They reduce the load that stays invisible until it is too late.

The working pack was 23. Five remain. Tom is named after a late brother. Sausage died during a police search that found nothing. Pepper’s son — Sausage’s grandson — is still here.

Sausage Therapy exists so other people can learn that before they lose the years it took to see it.
